What services does CareCarry offer? +

We offer Load/Unload, Pack/Unpack, Cleaning Help / Maid Services, and a Gun Safe Fee for specialty handling. You can combine any of these in a single booking.

Do you provide a moving truck? +

No. Moving Helpers do not provide moving trucks or supplies. You'll need to arrange your own rental truck, van, or trailer.

How does pricing work? +

Helpers are billed by the hour with a 2‑hour minimum. Two helpers cost $175/hr, three helpers $255/hr, and four helpers $335/hr. Cleaning starts at $95/hr. Gun safe handling is a flat $150 per safe.

What is Safeload℠ Coverage? +

Safeload is optional coverage you can add per service. Plans start at $14.99 for $2,500 of coverage and scale up to $39.99 for $10,000.

Can I change my booking after I submit it? +

Yes. Reach out via the contact details on your confirmation and we'll adjust the date, time, crew size, or hours as availability allows.
